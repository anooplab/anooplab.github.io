Computational chemistry · automated exploration · scientific machine learning

Tools, chemical space, and interpretable models for molecular discovery.

AnoopLab is currently based at Digital University Kerala, continuing a computational chemistry research programme developed across Digital University Kerala and IIT Kharagpur.

We develop computational methods for automated chemical exploration and scientific machine learning. Quantum chemistry, software and data are combined to study molecular structures, reactions and materials, with emphasis on chemical interpretation, validation and reproducible workflows.

01 · Research programme

Two connected directions

The programme asks two related methodological questions: how to explore chemical space systematically, and how to learn from scientific data without losing chemical meaning.

  1. 01

    Automated chemical exploration

    We develop software and search strategies to generate molecular structures, navigate reactions and explore complex chemical spaces with less manual bias. PyAR and reproducible workflows provide the infrastructure; reaction discovery, aggregates, nanoclusters and molecular design provide demanding applications. PyAR → Reaction discovery → Structures and clusters → Functional molecules and materials →

  2. 02

    Scientific machine learning

    We use machine learning to accelerate energy models, guide molecular search and reveal interpretable structure–property relationships. Models are judged by their chemical grounding, validation, uncertainty and domain limits—not by benchmark performance alone. See molecular-discovery applications →

How the directions connect. Automated exploration generates structures, reactions and data; scientific machine learning helps interpret and prioritise them. Both remain anchored in quantum-chemical validation and chemical reasoning.
